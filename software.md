# Software

Ham radio software and web services, grouped by what they do. Platform and
price are noted for each entry.

CW specific software (trainers, decoders) lives in [cw.md](cw.md).

## Logging — contest

- [N1MM Logger+](https://n1mmwp.hamdocs.com/) — the most used contest logger. CW, SSB, RTTY, digital. Windows, free
- [DXLog.net](https://www.dxlog.net/) — contest logger. CW, SSB, digital. Windows, paid
- [Win-Test](https://www.win-test.com/) — contest logger, strong for multi-operator. CW, SSB, digital. Windows, paid
- [WriteLog](https://writelog.com/) — contest logger. CW, SSB, RTTY, digital. Windows, paid
- [QARTest](https://www.ik3qar.it/software/qartest/en/) — contest logger. CW, SSB, digital. Windows, free
- [TR4W](https://tr4w.net/) — light contest logger. CW, SSB. Windows, free

All of the above upload to LoTW through integrations.

## Logging — general

- [Log4OM 2](https://www.log4om.com/) — full station log with digital mode integration. Windows, free. LoTW
- [Logger32](https://www.logger32.net/) — long established general log. Windows, free. LoTW
- [DXKeeper](https://www.dxlabsuite.com/dxkeeper/) — log from the DXLab suite. Windows, free. LoTW
- [QLog](https://www.qlog.co/) — modern general log with digital mode integration. Windows, free. LoTW
- [AC Log](https://www.n3fjp.com/aclog.html) — simple general log, easy to start with. Windows, paid. LoTW
- [Winlog32](http://www.winlog32.co.uk/) — general log. Windows, paid. LoTW

## DX spotting and alerts

- [DX Summit](http://dxsummit.fi/#/) — classic web DX cluster. Web, free
- [SpotCollector](https://www.dxlabsuite.com/spotcollector/) — collects spots from many clusters. Windows, free
- [HamAlert](https://hamalert.org/login) — tells your phone when a wanted station appears. Web and mobile, free and paid
- [DXView](http://www.dxlabsuite.com/dxview/) — shows where a DX station is, with beam heading. Windows, free

## Propagation

- [VOACAP Online](https://www.voacap.com/) — HF propagation prediction in the browser. Web, free
- [HamCAP](https://www.dxatlas.com/HamCap/) — VOACAP with a friendly interface. Windows, free
- [VOAProp](https://www.g4ilo.com/voaprop.html) — another VOACAP front end. Windows, free
- [HF+ Propagation Map](https://hf.dxview.org/) — live propagation map. Web, free

## Rig control

- [FLRig](https://sourceforge.net/projects/fldigi/files/flrig/) — CAT rig control, pairs with fldigi. Windows, free

## Station suites

- [DXLab Suite](https://www.dxlabsuite.com/) — logging, DX, propagation and rig control in one set. Windows, free. LoTW
- [Ham Radio Deluxe](https://www.hamradiodeluxe.com/) — logging, digital modes and rig control. Windows, paid. LoTW
