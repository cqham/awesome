# Reference

Band plans and other documents you look up rather than read once.

## Band plans

IARU Region 1 covers Europe, Africa, the Middle East and northern Asia. Your
national society may publish a stricter version — that one wins.

| Name | What it is | Link |
|---|---|---|
| IARU R1 HF band plan | Who uses what on 135 kHz to 29.7 MHz, including the CW segments | https://www.iaru-r1.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/hf_r1_bandplan.pdf |
| IARU R1 VHF and up band plan | Same for 50 MHz and above | https://www.iaru-r1.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/VHF-Bandplan.pdf |
| IARU R1 band plans — official page | Where to look for a newer version of the two PDFs above | https://www.iaru-r1.org/reference/band-plans/ |
| IARU R1 VHF and up band planning | Committee page with the latest VHF and up decisions | https://www.iaru-r1.org/about-us/committees-and-working-groups/vhf-uhf-shf-committee-c5/vhf-up-bandplanning/ |
