# CW (Morse code)

Resources for learning and practicing CW.

## Devices

- [HamHobby shop](https://hamhobby.odoo.com/shop) — keys, paddles and CW accessories

## Publications

- [ITU-R M.1677-1 — International Morse code](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/m/R-REC-M.1677-1-200910-I!!PDF-E.pdf) — the official standard
- [The Art and Skill of Radio-Telegraphy (N0HFF)](https://www.n9bor.us/images/pdf/n0hff_3.pdf) — classic book by William Pierpont
- [Poradnik CW — SP2MDS](https://xtreme69.nazwa.pl/sp2mds/Poradnik-cw-SP2MDS.pdf) — CW guide in Polish
